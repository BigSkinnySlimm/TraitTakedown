Privacy Policy for Trait Takedown

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

Zachary Howard ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Trait Takedown mobile application and web application. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our game.

1. Data We Collect and How We Use It

  • Usernames: You are required to create an "Agent Username" to play online. This username is stored locally on your device and is only broadcasted temporarily over PeerJS WebRTC connections to facilitate live matchmaking with friends. We do not store your username on any centralized database.
  • Local Game Data: Your coins, MMR (matchmaking rating), win records, unlocked rosters, and friends list are stored strictly on your local device (via LocalStorage and IndexedDB). We do not collect or harvest this telemetry.
  • In-App Purchases: If you purchase the Commander VIP Pass or coin packs, the transaction is processed securely by Google Play Billing or a third-party processor (Stripe, PayPal, Ko-fi). We do not collect, process, or store your credit card details or billing address. Google Play securely relays a digital entitlement receipt to your device to unlock the content.

2. Third-Party Services

The app utilizes the following third-party infrastructure which may collect technical data (like IP addresses) required for their services to function:

  • PeerJS: Used as a signaling server to connect you and your friends for P2P multiplayer.
  • Google Play Services: Used to securely process and verify in-app purchases and digital goods entitlements on Android.
  • GitHub Pages: Used to host the web application files securely via HTTPS.

3. Children's Privacy

Trait Takedown does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. Because all game data is stored locally and we operate no central player database, we do not possess the capacity to store children's data. If you are a parent and wish to delete your child's local game data, you may do so by clearing the app data in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
